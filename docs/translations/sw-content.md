# Kiswahili content — draft translations

> **Draft for native-speaker review.** These are working Kiswahili translations
> of the site's highest-visibility content, for a government (formal) register.
> Have a fluent Kiswahili reviewer check and adjust before publishing. Technical
> terms are kept close to common Tanzanian usage; where an English term is
> standard in industry, it is kept or glossed.

## How to use this

Two homes for Kiswahili text, matching how the site resolves locale:

1. **CMS content** (news, projects, publications, services, departments,
   technologies, vacancies, tenders): add a **Kiswahili (sw)** localization for
   each entry in the Strapi admin and paste the translated title/summary/body
   there. The frontend overlays the sw fields per entry and falls back to
   English for anything not yet translated (`docs/I18N.md`).
2. **Bundled fallback / static chrome** already in `messages/sw.json` (nav,
   footer, common UI) — extend there for any static string not stored in the CMS.

The UI chrome (menus, buttons, footer) is already translated. This file covers
the *content*.

## Section intros

**Laboratory**

> Maabara maalum za TIRDO zinazozingatia viwango hutoa huduma kwa viwanda,
> serikali, wajasiriamali wadogo na umma. Upimaji unahusu chakula na wadudu,
> kemia ya viwanda, vifaa, nishati na mazingira — ukiunga mkono uthibitisho wa
> ubora, ufuasi wa kanuni na uendelezaji wa bidhaa.
>
> Sampuli na majibu husimamiwa kupitia NILIMS, Mfumo wa Kitaifa wa Usimamizi wa
> Taarifa za Maabara za Viwanda wa TIRDO, unaowawezesha wateja kusajili sampuli,
> kufuatilia hatua na kupokea majibu mtandaoni.

**Consultancy**

> TIRDO ni mshirika wa kiufundi wa fani mbalimbali kwa viwanda, serikali na
> washirika wa maendeleo — ukichanganya maabara zilizoidhinishwa, utaalamu wa
> uhandisi na uzoefu wa miaka mingi wa uwandani kutatua changamoto halisi za
> viwanda.
>
> Kazi za ushauri huombwa na kusimamiwa kupitia CIAP, Lango la Ushauri na
> Ushauri wa Viwanda la TIRDO, linaloshughulikia mchakato kuanzia ombi la mteja
> hadi kukamilika.

**Training**

> TIRDO hutoa kozi fupi maalum na programu za ujenzi wa uwezo kwa wenye viwanda,
> wajasiriamali wadogo na umma, ukichanganya uwezo wa maabara na uzoefu wa
> uwandani kujenga ujuzi unaoboresha uzalishaji, ubora na usalama.
>
> Kozi huchapishwa, na usajili husimamiwa, kupitia TeLTP — Jukwaa la TIRDO la
> Kujifunza na Mafunzo Mtandaoni — linalohifadhi ratiba, usajili na nyenzo za
