# Public website user guide

A short guide to using the TIRDO website. For content management see
`docs/ADMIN-MANUAL.md`.

## Navigation

The top menu groups the site: **About Us** (mandate, governance, organization
structure, board, administration, COMSATS), **Our Work / Departments**
(research, engineering and technology divisions), **Services**, **Research &
Innovation** (projects, T-Hub, publications), **Media Centre** (news, events,
gallery, documents), **Analytics**, and **Contact Us**. The menu stays pinned to
the top as you scroll.

## Language

Use the **English / Kiswahili** switch in the top bar to change the interface
language. Your choice is remembered on your device. (Menus, headings and labels
switch language; some long articles are currently English only.)

## Search

Click the **search icon** (or open `/search`). Type at least two characters to
see **suggestions**; press Enter for full results. Results can be **filtered by
type** (News, Publications, Projects, Services, Departments, Vacancies,
Tenders), and matching words are highlighted. The address bar keeps your query,
so results are shareable and the back button works.

## Browsing content

- **Projects** (`/projects`) — filter by **research area** and **status**
  (Featured / Ongoing / Completed).
- **Publications** (`/publications`) — filter by **type** and **year**, or
  search titles/authors/keywords; expand a row to see the abstract, keywords,
  DOI and citation, and download the PDF.
- **Careers** (`/careers`) — open vacancies with category filters; closed
  positions are hidden by default (toggle **Include closed** to see them). Use
  **Apply** to reach the Government recruitment portal.
- **Tenders** (`/tenders`) — active procurement notices with reference numbers
  and documents; closed tenders archive automatically after their deadline.

## Contact

The **Contact Us** page has TIRDO's address, phone and email, and an enquiry
form. Fill in your name, email, subject and message and submit; the enquiry is
sent to the TIRDO team.

## Accessibility

The site targets **WCAG 2.1 AA**: a "Skip to content" link, keyboard-navigable
menus, visible focus, sufficient colour contrast, and reduced-motion support
(the homepage carousel does not auto-advance and offers a pause control if you
prefer reduced motion). See `docs/ACCESSIBILITY.md`.

## Downloads

Publications, documents and tender files open or download as PDFs. Right-click →
"Save link as" to save a copy.
